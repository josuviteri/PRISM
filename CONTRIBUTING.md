# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for even thinking about contributing to this project! We truly appreciate your enthusiasm, your time, and your effort.

However, before you open a pull request, please read the following guidelines very carefully.

---

## Rule #1: The Golden Rule

**No pull requests will be merged. Ever.**

In fact, they won't even be read. 

### Why?
* **It’s perfect as it is:** (It’s definitely not, but we like to pretend).
* **Trust issues:** We have a strict "if we didn't write it, it doesn't exist" philosophy.
* **Maintainer peace:** We are currently at 0 merge conflicts (4 now), and we'd love to keep it that way forever.

---

## How to Contribute (Without PRs)

Since pull requests are automatically sent to the digital void, here is how you can actually contribute:

1. **Star the repository:** This increases our dopamine levels.
2. **Open an Issue:** Feel free to report bugs or request features. We can't promise we'll fix or add them, but we promise to admire the formatting.
3. **Fork the repo:** Want to fix a bug or add a feature? Fork it! Build your own empire where *you* refuse everyone else's PRs.
4. **Telepathy:** Send your suggestions directly to the maintainer's brain. (Success rate may vary).

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: But I found a critical security vulnerability and fixed it!**  
*A: Wow, incredible work! Feel free to enjoy that fix on your local machine or fork.*

**Q: What if I open a PR anyway?**  
*A: It will join the ancient ruins of unreviewed code, sitting silently in the dark corners of GitHub.*

**Q: Is there any exception?**  
*A: No.*

---

Thank you for understanding, and happy coding on your own forks!
